# Changekit

How change packages work in this repository.

- **changekit:** 0.4.0
- **changes:** changes/
- **specs:** specs/
- **language:** English
- **validate:** `npm test`
- **commit:** conventional, one per group, after explicit approval

## House rules

- `specs/index.md` is the route table. A new behavior gets a row there.
- Every spec carries a `Status:` line under its title.
